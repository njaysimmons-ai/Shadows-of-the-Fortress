# Living NPCs: dialogue
#
# Edit freely. In game, press Ctrl+F8 to reload this file without restarting.
#
# Each [section] holds lines; one is picked at random. Tokens filled in when spoken:
#   {player} your name     {class} your class    {level} your level
#   {npc}    the speaker   {zone}  scene name    {time}  morning/afternoon/evening/night
#
# GREETINGS are added to the front of an NPC's normal reply when you hail them.
#   greet.first          never spoken to this character before
#   greet.returning      spoken a few times
#   greet.regular        spoken often (see RegularAfterHails in the config)
#   greet.long_absence   you've been away from the game for a while (see AbsenceDays)
#   greet.hurt           you're below 35% health
#   greet.<role>.<familiarity>   role is vendor, guard, banker or townsfolk
#   flavor.time.<time>   sometimes tacked on after the greeting
#   flavor.class.<Class> same, by your class name (e.g. flavor.class.Paladin)
#   hostile              replaces the "..." from NPCs who dislike you
#   busy                 replaces "I'm busy right now..."
#
# AMBIENT lines are spoken on their own by friendly NPCs near you.
#   ambient                      anyone, anywhere
#   ambient.<role>               ambient.vendor, ambient.guard, ambient.banker, ambient.townsfolk
#   ambient.time.<time>          by time of day
#   ambient.zone.<Zone>          by scene; the BepInEx log prints the name each time you zone
#   ambient.to_player            said to you when you stand close
#   ambient.player_hurt          said to you when you stand close and are badly hurt
#
# ONE NPC: give a single character their own voice. Use their exact name.
#   [npc.Some Name.greet]   [npc.Some Name.greet.first]   [npc.Some Name.ambient]
#   [npc.Some Name.hurt]    [npc.Some Name.hostile]       [npc.Some Name.busy]

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ greetings

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ ambient

[ambient]
*hums an old tune under their breath*
*stretches and rolls their shoulders*
Hm. Where did I put that?
Rumour says something big is stirring in the deep places.
They say the old ruins are full of treasure. They say a lot of things.
*glances at the sky*
Another day, another copper.
*mutters something about the price of bread*
My knees aren't what they used to be.
Did you hear? Someone came back from the wilds with a sword that glowed.
I keep having the same dream. Water, and a door that won't open.
*yawns*
Adventurers. Always rushing about.

[ambient.time.morning]
Nothing like the morning air.
*sips from a steaming cup*
Up and at it.

[ambient.time.afternoon]
*wipes sweat from their brow*
The day drags on.

[ambient.time.evening]
Almost time to close up.
Evening already? Where does the day go?
*watches the sunset for a moment*

[ambient.time.night]
*shivers* Cold tonight.
Did you hear that? ...Must be the wind.
Should be in bed by now.
I don't like the dark out here.

[ambient.vendor]
Fine goods! Fair prices! Come and see!
Everything must go! Well, not everything. But some things.
Bandages, rations, rope! Don't go into the wilds without rope!
I've got just the thing for you, whoever you are.
*rearranges the wares for the third time*
Business has been slow. Don't suppose you're buying?

[ambient.guard]
*scans the horizon*
All quiet. Too quiet, maybe.
Another long shift.
*shifts weight from one foot to the other*
If those brigands come back, they'll find us ready.
Keep moving, nothing to see here.
I could do with a hot meal and a soft bed.

[ambient.guard.night]
Night watch is the worst watch.
*peers into the darkness*
Stay sharp. Things move at night.

[ambient.banker]
*counts coins under their breath*
Every copper accounted for. Every single one.
Interest rates? Don't get me started.
