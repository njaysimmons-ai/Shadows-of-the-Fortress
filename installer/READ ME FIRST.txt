LIVING NPCs for Erenshor
=======================

TO INSTALL
  1. Unzip this whole folder anywhere (for example, your Desktop).
  2. Double-click "Install Living NPCs.bat".
  3. Answer the questions. It finds your Erenshor folder, installs BepInEx (the mod loader)
     if you don't have it, installs the mod, and can set up AI chat.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
The installer is a plain-text script; open Install-LivingNPCs.ps1 in Notepad to read it.

TO UPDATE
  Run the installer again from the new version's folder.

IF AI CHAT WON'T CONNECT
  Double-click "Check AI.bat". It checks each step, fixes what it can, and saves a report
  to your Desktop (LivingNPCs-AI-report.txt).

TO UNINSTALL
  Double-click "Uninstall Living NPCs.bat".

IN GAME
  /emotes     list emotes (/wave, /bow, /cheer, /hug, /dance, ...)
  /friends    your closest friendships      /memories Name   what someone remembers about you
  /find Name  a mist trail leads you to someone in this zone (/find lists who's here, /find stop)
  /ai         AI chat status. Target a sim (or stand next to one) and type to talk.
  /ai test    check the AI is working
  /p message  talk to your group      /w Name message   whisper a sim
  /hair       change hairstyle (/hair prev, /bald); Bald is also in character creation
  Ctrl+F8     reload the dialogue files after editing them

Your settings and all the dialogue files are in Erenshor\BepInEx\config\
